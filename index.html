Happy seven-monthsary, my love. 💌

Seven months may look like just a number to the world, but to me, it holds so many feelings, so many little memories, so many moments when you crossed my mind and made me smile without even knowing it.

Some days, I miss you more than I can explain. Sometimes I wait for a message, replay a conversation in my head, or wish I could borrow just a little more time with you. That's how precious our little moments have become to me.

I don't promise that every day will be perfect. I promise that your feelings matter to me, that I'll keep trying to understand you, and that I want us to be able to talk through the hard moments with kindness. I want to celebrate your happiness, comfort you when life feels heavy, laugh with you over the silliest things, and keep making memories that belong only to us.

Thank you for being the Princess in my fairytale — not because you're from a perfect story, but because being close to you makes this real life feel a little more magical.

Today is our seventh month, but my favourite part isn't counting how long we've been together. It's knowing that there's still so much of you to discover, so many stories to share, and so many tomorrows we can look forward to.

So, my Princess, whenever you read this, imagine me holding your hand beneath a sky full of stars and telling you softly: I'm so glad it's you. ❤️

Forever your Prince,
